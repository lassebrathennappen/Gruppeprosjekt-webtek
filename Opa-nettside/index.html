Administrative details

This document describes the project requirements for the website of OPA!, a modern Greek restaurant located in Trondheim. The client is the restaurant owner Lucas, who is responsible for the restaurant’s overall operations, including food service, staffing, and customer experience.

Audience

The intended audience will be people who are interested in dining at OPA!, website includes students, young adults and local residents in Trondheim. It will also include tourists visiting the city who are interested in experiencing Greek-inspired cuisine. These users will mainly use the website to view the menu, find location information, and make reservations.

The website will also be used by customers who already know the restaurant and want quick access to opening hours, contact details, menu or booking options.
