Administrative details

This document describes the project requirements for the website of OPA!, a modern Greek restaurant located in Trondheim. The client is the restaurant owner Lucas, who is responsible for the restaurant’s overall operations, including food service, staffing, and customer experience.

Purpouse and Goals

The purpouse of this website is to represent Opa, a traditional greek restaurant in Trondheim and get more people to eat there

The first goal is to get more costumers. The good navigation and accesibility will give a good impression and make it easier to view both the food and price class to ensure the costumers know what they are getting into. It will also be easy to book a table through the contact section of the website, which in turn will attract more costumers.

Our second goal is to make the website easy to find. Every page will have a clear title and description, and the website will mention Greek food and Trondheim, so that the website shows up when people search on web browsers.
