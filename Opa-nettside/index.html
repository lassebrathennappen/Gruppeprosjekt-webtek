Administrative details

This document describes the project requirements for the website of OPA!, a modern Greek restaurant located in Trondheim. The client is the restaurant owner Lucas, who is responsible for the restaurant’s overall operations, including food service, staffing, and customer experience. Lucas recently moved to Trondheim and is originally from Greece. He previously worked as a chef at a restaurant in Bergen, and now wants to pursue his goal of opening his own restaurant.

Purpose and Goals

The purpose of this website is to represent Opa, a traditional greek restaurant in Trondheim and get more people to eat there

The first goal is to get more costumers. The good navigation and accesibility will give a good impression and make it easier to view both the food and price class to ensure the costumers know what they are getting into. It will also be easy to book a table through the contact section of the website, which in turn will attract more costumers.

Our second goal is to make the website easy to find. Every page will have a clear title and description, and the website will mention Greek food and Trondheim, so that the website shows up when people search on web browsers.

Audience

The intended audience will be people who are interested in dining at OPA!, website includes students, young adults and local residents in Trondheim. It will also include tourists visiting the city who are interested in experiencing Greek-inspired cuisine. These users will mainly use the website to view the menu, find location information, and make reservations.

The website will also be used by customers who already know the restaurant and want quick access to opening hours, contact details, menu or booking options.

The content of the site and how it is organized

The website for OPA! will be structured into four main pages: Home, Menu, Booking, and Contact. The structure is designed to make it easy for users to find relevant information quickly and efficiently.

Home

The homepage will function as an introduction to OPA! and provide users with a first impression of the restaurant. It will include a short description of the concept and a brief background of the restaurant as a modern Greek restaurant in Trondheim. The page will also present visual content such as images of the restaurant atmosphere and selected dishes to create interest and reflect the Greek-inspired style.

In addition, the homepage includes highlights such as news or updates, for example student discounts, new menu items, or featured dishes. It will also provide a clear shortcut to the booking page to support reservations, while essential contact information will be available at the bottom of the page for easy access.

Menu

This page will present the restaurant’s food and drink offerings and their prices. The menu will be organized into categories such as starters, main courses, desserts, and drinks.

Booking

The booking page will allow users to reserve a table through an online form. To fill out the form, users enter information such as name, date, time, and number of guests. After submitting the form, the user will receive feedback confirming that their reservation request has been received, and that they will receive a confirmation email to the provided email address to acknowledge the booking request.

About us

The "About us" page will include two pages: about us and contact. The "About us" page will include information about the restaurant’s concept, background, and inspiration. It will present OPA! as a modern Greek restaurant in Trondheim and briefly describe its origins and culinary style. The "contact" will include essential information such as address, opening hours, phone number, and email.

Footer

Adress, opening hours, contact information, social media

Functional and Non-Functional Requirements

Functional requirements describe what the website should do, and non-functional requirements describe how well it should do it

Functional requirements

  1. The website shows Opa's menu with prices
  2. The website shows address, opening hours and the restaurants contact information
  3. The user must be able to find out how to book a tabel relatively easy

Non-Functional requirements

  1. The website should work well on both computers and mobilde devices
  2. The website should be usable for people who use screen readers
  3. The menu should be available from any page of the website with one click
